# the clean fixture: staging plan

## Intro
focus: `lab` -- the labelled number the scene keeps coming back to

> The [p]picture comes first. Then the [e]equation, and the [n]number.

p  new: the picture is set down on the left  -- the scene is about a thing, before it is about a formula
e  new: the equation is written beside it  -- the formula describes the picture next to it
n  new: the number is written under the equation  -- the formula has one answer, and this is it

> Now we [i]point at it again.

i  mark: the number is named again  -- the answer is what the rest of the scene refers back to

> We [s]simplify the fraction, and a factor of two alone is not a step.

s  side: the fraction collapses to a half  -- the halving is a cancellation, not a new quantity
